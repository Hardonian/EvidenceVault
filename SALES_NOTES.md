# Sales Notes
## 5-minute founder demo
1. Open `/app` and show onboarding + templates.
2. Create template evidence and show urgency queue.
3. Generate review snapshot and show health delta.
4. Generate proofpack and show history.
5. Close on weekly cadence + owner accountability.

## Pilot qualification questions
- Are you tracking evidence in spreadsheets today?
- Do you miss renewal windows or owner assignments?
- Can your team run a weekly 15-minute review?

## Pain indicators
- Expired docs during sales/security reviews.
- No proof history for customer requests.
- Repeated owner follow-up friction.

## Anti-targets
- Teams needing enterprise workflow orchestration.
- Teams expecting compliance certification automation.

## Monetization and activation scoring
- Track activation milestones and maturity stage in app founder panel.
- Use recurring review cadence + unresolved trend to identify friction and conversion readiness.
- Ask pain discovery: What recurring operational fire keeps coming back weekly? Which owner handoffs fail today?
- Ask upgrade readiness: Are multiple owners contributing weekly? Is proofpack history now critical to external trust?
