# Founder Guide

Purpose: Deterministic founder-facing view of value, continuity loop, and demo/proof path.
Audience: Founders/evaluators.
Canonical status: Founder-facing canonical doc.

## What EvidenceVault Is
A deterministic operations system that turns weekly review discipline into continuity history and portable exports.

## 4-Week Continuity Ritual
- Week 1: create first review snapshot.
- Week 2: run second snapshot and compare latest vs previous.
- Week 3: continue cadence; friction patterns become continuity evidence.
- Week 4: export narratives + comparison as founder proof artifacts.

## Founder Outcomes
- Demonstrable review consistency.
- Historical comparison grounded in persisted truth.
- Exportable operating narrative for stakeholders.

## Limitations / Truth Statements
- No AI recommendations.
- No compliance certification/legal conclusion engine.
- Value depends on consistent weekly operation.
