Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from Vanta or Drata?
We're built for smaller teams. Vanta and Drata are full GRC platforms designed for enterprises — they start at $600-1,500/mo and take 2-4 weeks to set up. EvidenceVault focuses on the evidence lifecycle: upload, review, export. Most teams go from signup to first proofpack in under 15 minutes. At $49/mo, we're 10x more accessible.
Can I self-host EvidenceVault?
Yes. EvidenceVault runs as a Docker container. Deploy on your own infrastructure — AWS, GCP, Azure, or on-prem. Your evidence data never touches our servers. The Enterprise tier includes self-hosted deployment with SLA and dedicated support.
What compliance frameworks do you support?
SOC 2 (all trust service criteria), ISO 27001, HIPAA, SOC 3, and custom frameworks. Map evidence items to multiple frameworks simultaneously — upload once, use everywhere. New frameworks are added based on customer demand.
What if I need to cancel? Do I lose my evidence?
Never. Export your proofpack anytime — it's a portable PDF or XLSX with verification hashes that works outside EvidenceVault. Your evidence is yours, not locked in our platform. Cancel anytime, no questions asked.
How long does setup take?
Most teams upload their first evidence item in under 5 minutes. Setup: create an account, define your controls, start uploading. For our pilot program, we'll get you fully set up in a 30-minute kickoff call. No training required.
