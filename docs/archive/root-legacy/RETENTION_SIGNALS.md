# Retention Signals

EvidenceVault retention signals are operational, not behavioral analytics:
- second weekly review completed
- review streak continuity
- unresolved trend direction
- overdue/expiring trend
- ownerless evidence trend
- days since last upload/proofpack activity
- operational drift warnings when cadence breaks

Upgrade readiness is surfaced with plain heuristics: evidence depth, multiple owners, recurring reviews, proofpack history growth, and streak durability.
